# ADA Run

An endless runner game in the style of Subway Surfers and Temple Run, set on the ADA University campus in Baku.

You play an ADA student running across campus to escape a giant **F** (Academic Fail). Dodge the distractions that make students fail, like TikTok, Instagram, parties and missed deadlines. Collect books to earn points. Reach **100 points** to pass your degree and get your diploma.

## How to play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser on a computer or phone. Nothing needs to be installed.

| Action | Keyboard | Phone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Change lane / turn at corners | ← → or A D | Swipe left / right |
| Jump | ↑, W or Space | Swipe up |
| Slide | ↓, S or Shift | Swipe down |
| Hoverboard | E | Double-tap |
| Pause | P or Esc | Pause button |

## Features

- **Real ADA campus.** The 3D world is built from photos and the real names of ADA buildings: Buildings A, B, D, E and L, the Library, the Student Center, the Caravanserai (School of Business), ADA Gardens and ADA School.
- **Your major, your courses.** All 30 bachelor's and master's programmes from the ADA academic catalogue are included, each with its own course list. Every course you pass ends with a short quiz about a real topic from that course.
- **Turns and obstacles.**
  - The track is a curved world with 90° turns.
  - Every turn has a warning zone first: chevron signs, caution cones and neon barriers.
  - Obstacles include hurdles to jump, banners to slide under and parked carts.
  - Moving hazards include a swinging exam clock and a sliding pop-quiz barrier.
- **Fair by design.** Obstacles come from pattern templates that never block all three lanes with something you can't jump or slide past. Gaps grow as you speed up.
- **Scoring.** Combos (x2, x3, x5) and pomegranate books worth 1.00 point. Power-ups: hoverboard, jetpack, coffee magnet, focus mode and super-jump shoes.
- **Your student.** Choose a boy or girl, skin tone, hair, clothes and accessories, all shown on an ADA student ID card.
- **Diploma.** At the end you get a transcript with grades for each course and a GPA.
- **Look.** Caspian Blue, White and Pomegranate visual identity.
